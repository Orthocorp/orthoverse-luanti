Every token is a land

Each revealed Orthoverse token is a square of the world, 96 blocks a side. Its token ID shapes the hills, lakes, woods and quarries; its castle level decides the castle. These are the tokens as marketplaces show them, drawn from the world as it stands, so whatever a holder builds shows on their token.

    Build on the crofts, or your own castle

    Crofts, the lands with no castle yet, are common ground: sign in with any Ethereum wallet and mine and build there, token or not. A land with a castle is its holder's alone, and they build there in creative mode, with every block in the game.

    Travel by henge

    Nine stone circles stand at the hearts of nine island kingdoms. Walk through a gateway and you come out at the next henge along, north, south, east or west.

    Two realms

    Some castles belong to the futuristic realm. There, tarmac, glass domes and towers take the place of meadows and keeps.

    Walk anywhere

    Anyone may wander the whole Orthoverse, as under the Finnish everyman's right. Building is for each land's holder. Find any land by its name or token ID on the map.

    Getting in

    1. Open the game

      Go to orthoverse.land/play on a desktop or laptop. The game runs in the browser, with nothing to install.

    2. Sign in

      Press Sign in at the top of any page, connect your wallet and choose a player name, which stays with that wallet. No wallet? Play as a guest and explore.

    3. Find your land

      Your lands are on your Account page, where you can also reveal your own. In the game, press T and type /home to go there.
