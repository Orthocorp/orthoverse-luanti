Adventures

Holders build adventures on their lands: dungeons, towers and caves to get through, alone or with friends. Go in through the adventure's gate in the game; reach the finish to get on its table. One party at a time: if a party is inside, wait at the gate until they come out.

Times count from the gate to the finish. Each saved version of an adventure has its own records.

No adventures are open yet. Holders of a land at castle level 5 or more can build one: /adventure in the game explains how.
