The Orthoverse

Every land is an Orthoverse token. Walk them in your browser.

Names are case sensitive.
  • New here? Choose Register to create your player. After that, use Log in.
  • Best on a desktop or laptop with a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox. The first visit downloads the game engine (tens of megabytes); later visits load it from your browser.
  • Walk with W A S D, jump with space, open your inventory with I, chat with T. Type /whereami in chat to see whose land you are on.
