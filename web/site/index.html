The Orthoverse

Every Ethereum address has an Orthoverse token. Every revealed token is a land, shaped by its token ID, in a voxel world you can walk.

Play in your browser Explore the map

What is here

Nine island kingdoms, one round each henge, with the lands of the tokens revealed so far. A land's hills, lakes, woods and quarries come from its token ID; its level decides its castle, and levels 8 and up flip it into the tarmac realm.

The world runs on Luanti, the open-source voxel engine, with Minetest Game: grow, dig, craft and build. Anyone may wander anywhere, as under the Finnish everyman's right; building is for a land's owner.

Playing

The browser version needs a desktop or laptop with a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox. The desktop Luanti app will be able to join too, once the server is open to it.
