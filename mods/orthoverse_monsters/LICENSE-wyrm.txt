The wyrm (The Wyrm Below's dragon): its model, textures, fire and smoke
particles and sounds come from Draconis by ElCeejo
(https://content.luanti.org/packages/ElCeejo/draconis/,
https://github.com/ElCeejo/draconis), renamed:

  models/orthoverse_monsters_wyrm.b3d          draconis_fire_dragon.b3d
  textures/orthoverse_monsters_wyrm.png        draconis_fire_dragon_red.png
  textures/orthoverse_monsters_wyrm_shading.png  draconis_baked_in_shading.png
  textures/orthoverse_monsters_wyrm_eyes.png   draconis_fire_eyes_orange.png
  textures/orthoverse_monsters_wyrm_fire.png   draconis_fire_particle.png
  textures/orthoverse_monsters_wyrm_smoke.png  draconis_smoke_particle.png
  sounds/orthoverse_monsters_wyrm_roar.1-3.ogg draconis_fire_dragon.1-3.ogg
  sounds/orthoverse_monsters_wyrm_flap.ogg     draconis_flap.ogg
  sounds/orthoverse_monsters_wyrm_breath.ogg   draconis_fire_breath.ogg
  sounds/orthoverse_monsters_wyrm_slam.ogg     draconis_slam.ogg
  sounds/orthoverse_monsters_wyrm_repel.ogg    draconis_repel.ogg

Its behaviour (orthoverse_monsters/init.lua) is our own.
