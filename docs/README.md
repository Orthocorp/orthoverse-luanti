# Orthoverse design documents

Plans, decisions and research behind the game, written while building it. Running the server is covered in [docker/README.md](../docker/README.md).

## The world

- [land-abilities.md](land-abilities.md): what each castle level gives a holder on their land, and the principles for changing that.
- [accounts-plan.md](accounts-plan.md): password characters alongside wallets (built in 1002 to 1005).
- [old-code-findings.md](old-code-findings.md): the old flying-squid Orthoverse: world geometry and how lands were generated (historical).

## Adventures

- [adventures.md](adventures.md): **what exists now**, for players and builders.
- [adventure-decisions.md](adventure-decisions.md): the 39 decisions (all suggested defaults accepted) and progress on the branch.
- [dungeons-plan.md](dungeons-plan.md): the full plan: existing mods, phases, combat, character levels, parties, death, great monsters.
- [adventure-runs.md](adventure-runs.md): one party at a time or private copies, resets, league tables, the adventurer's kit, awkward cases.
- [adventures-review.md](adventures-review.md): a critical review of the plan and the changes it led to.
- [ideas-assessment.md](ideas-assessment.md): each idea assessed, and the milestones.
- [combat-numbers.md](combat-numbers.md): first numbers for weapons, armour, ranks and experience.
- [adventure-design.md](adventure-design.md): what makes D&D modules popular, and the four sample adventures (The Three Bells first).
- [builder-workflow.md](builder-workflow.md): an early sketch of how a holder builds an adventure.
- [monster-catalogue.md](monster-catalogue.md): ready-made Luanti monster packs and their licences.
