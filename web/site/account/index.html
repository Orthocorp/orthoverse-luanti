Account

Your character, your Orthoverse lands, and what you can do with them on the chain.

Sign in

Sign in with your player name and password, or with your Ethereum wallet. With a wallet you see your lands here, and if your address has no land yet, you can reveal it.

Your look

How your character looks in the game. Drag the figure to turn it round. When you save, it is what you wear in the game within a few seconds, and what everyone there sees.

Your lands

This wallet holds no Orthoverse lands. You can still mine and build on the crofts in the game, reveal your own land, give someone a land or give a castle upgrade to any land on the Blockchain tab.

Lands belong to Ethereum wallets. Link a wallet to your character (on the Blockchain tab) to see and build on the lands it holds, or to reveal your own.

Payments go to the Orthoverse contract from your own wallet; this site never holds your keys or your ETH.

Your wallet

Your wallet is on another network. Lands live on Ethereum mainnet.

Your own land

Every Ethereum address has an Orthoverse land waiting for it, and yours has not been revealed yet. Reveal it and it is placed in the world within a few minutes, with its own name: a croft at first, common land until you give it a castle.

Your land is on its way

Your address's land has been revealed on the chain, and is being placed in the world. This usually takes a minute or two; it will appear on the Lands tab.

Any land

Anyone may pay for a castle upgrade on any land, as a gift. Only a land's holder can flip it.

    Give someone a land

    Reveal the land of any Ethereum address, as a present: it becomes theirs, placed in the world within a few minutes.
