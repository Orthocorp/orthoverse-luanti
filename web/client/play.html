Every land is an Orthoverse token. Walk them in your browser.

Sign in to play

Sign in with your name and password, or with an Ethereum wallet, and your character keeps what you carry and achieve. Holders build on their own lands; anyone signed in may mine and build on the crofts, the common lands (characters without a wallet after their first hour of play).

Choose your player name

You are signed in. Choose the name you will have in the game.

Play as a guest

Explore, ride and travel by the henges. Guests cannot build.

  • Best on a desktop or laptop with a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox. The first visit downloads the game engine (tens of megabytes); later visits load it from your browser.
  • Walk with W A S D, jump with space, open your inventory with I, chat with T. /whereami tells you whose land you are on, /mylands lists yours.
  • /home takes you to your own land.
