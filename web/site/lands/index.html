Any land

Anyone may pay for a castle upgrade on any land, as a gift. Only a land's holder can flip it.

    Upgrades and flips are paid to the Orthoverse contract (0x118a…8922). The game rebuilds a land a minute or two after its level changes, keeping every tile anyone has built on; its token picture follows within a day.
